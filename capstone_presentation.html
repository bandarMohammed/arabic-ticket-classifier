1 · Problem & success metric

Route every ticket to the right queue —
in English and Arabic

The problem

Customers write in English, Modern Standard Arabic and Gulf dialect. The course baseline understands English well but fails on Arabic.

Our system

Takes a customer message → produces one of 27 intents + confidence, a route (auto / human), category and priority.

Measured by

Macro-F1 on held-out test sets, sliced by language and dialect (EN · MSA · Gulf), with 95% bootstrap CIs.

8 · Lessons & next steps

The bottleneck was data, not the model

Lessons

  • Slice your metrics — the overall score hid the Arabic failure.
  • Same frozen encoder + Arabic data = ×3 on Arabic.
  • Check for leakage — and report it.
  • Abstaining beats guessing.

Next steps

  • Collect real Gulf-dialect tickets
  • Typo & dialect augmentation
  • Cascade: low-confidence → LLM → human
  • Fine-tune once real Arabic data exists
